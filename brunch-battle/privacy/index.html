Brunch Battle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026. App: Brunch Battle (com.swindie.brunchbattle) by swindie.

Brunch Battle is a board game for Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: the game does not collect your personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not send gameplay data to any server of ours.

What stays on your device

Your saved games, settings and a note of which optional items you own are stored on your device only. We cannot see them. They are removed when you uninstall the app. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your Google backup; that is handled by Google under your own Google settings.

Purchases

The app offers optional one-time purchases (cosmetic items and supporter looks) through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details, name or email address. The app only asks Google Play whether you own an item, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device.

App review prompt

After you have played a few games, the app may ask Google Play to show its standard rating dialog. That dialog is shown and handled by Google Play. We do not receive your rating through the app or any personal information from it.

Optional feedback form

The app can link to an anonymous feedback form hosted by Tally (tally.so). The link opens in your web browser. The form does not ask for your email address, and the app itself sends nothing to it. Please do not put personal information in your feedback. Tally's own privacy policy applies to the form.

Internet access

The game is fully playable offline. The app contains the permission Android requires for Google Play purchases and for opening links, but it does not use the internet to collect or upload your information.

Children

Brunch Battle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
